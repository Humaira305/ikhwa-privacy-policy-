# Ikhwa Quran Institute – Account Deletion Request Ikhwa Quran Institute users can request the deletion of their app account and associated personal data. ## How to Request Account Deletion To request deletion of your account, send an email to: **ikhwainternational@gmail.com** Email subject: **Account Deletion Request** Please include your registered email address and name so we can verify your account. ## Data That Will Be Deleted After verification, we will delete: - App account and profile information - Name, email address and phone number - Course enrollment information - Learning progress and attendance records - Quiz results and associated learning records ## Data Retention Personal account data and associated records will be deleted from our active Firebase records within 30 days of receiving a verified request. We may retain limited information only where required by applicable law or for legitimate security purposes, for the period required by law. ## Processing Time Verified account deletion requests will be processed within 30 days. **Developer:** Ikhwa Quran Institute International **Contact Email:** ikhwainternational@gmail.com
